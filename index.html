Cross River

Cross River State is a state in southeastern Nigeria, known for its beautiful natural environment, rich cultural heritage, and tourist attractions. Its capital city is Calabar, a historic city famous for its annual Calabar Carnival. The state is home to the Cross River National Park, waterfalls, mountains, and diverse wildlife. Cross River is also known for its friendly people, traditional festivals, and cultural diversity.
