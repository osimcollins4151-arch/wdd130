Every trip includes experienced guides, rafts, paddles, helmets, personal flotation devices, and a safety briefing.

Meadow Run Float

Ages: 6+ | Duration: Half Day

A relaxing introduction to rafting. Float through scenic valleys, practice easy paddle strokes, and enjoy gentle Class I–II rapids. This is a great choice for families and first-time rafters.

  • 2.5 hours on the river
  • Class I–II rapids
  • Great for families

Canyon Current

Ages: 12+ | Duration: Full Day

Our signature one-day adventure combines exciting rapids with beautiful canyon scenery. Stop for a riverside lunch before taking on lively Class II–III wave trains in the afternoon.

  • 5 hours on the river
  • Class II–III rapids
  • Lunch and transportation included

Wild Gorge Expedition

Ages: 16+ | Duration: 3 Days

Escape into the back country on a three-day rafting expedition. Challenge Class III–IV rapids during the day, then camp beside the river and enjoy meals under the stars each evening.

  • Two nights of riverside camping
  • Class III–IV rapids
  • Meals and camp equipment included
